PENGUIN vs FISH

Brave the ice maze, outswim the fish, and gather rewards!

Instructions

  • Collect krill for points and ice crystals to freeze fish so you can eat them.
  • Eat all four fish to clear the level and unlock a new maze.
  • Find a shield emblem every other level. Each gives 5 shield, up to 20; a hit costs 5, and a hit with no shield costs a life.
  • Collect Golden Krill to buy permanent upgrades in the Igloo Shop.
  • Earn up to 3 stars: clear the level, finish under 90 seconds, and collect every krill without taking a hit.
  • Survival: the maze shifts after every four fish, while fish gain speed with each maze. Keep going until all lives are lost.
  • Each level also rolls one random challenge: crosswind gusts, a glowing night maze, or a fish frenzy with more fish and ice crystals.
